Facilitating students since 2010

SA Global Education has been a leader in the higher education and visa industry for 10+ years, helping 500+ students achieve their dreams of studying abroad.

Making study abroad clear and achievable

Entry requirements, visa rules and funding options change often, and a single mistake can delay a student's plans by a year.

Our mission is to give every student an accurate picture of the courses they qualify for, the full cost of study and the opportunities available after graduation.

  • Each student's interests guide every recommendation
  • Every recommendation comes with a clear explanation
  • Advice reflects the latest admissions and visa regulations
  • Families are kept informed alongside the applicant
  • Support continues after students arrive abroad

A consultancy families trust and recommend

Our vision is to be the education consultancy that families across Pakistan and the United Kingdom trust with their children's futures, and recommend to relatives and friends for years to come.

What we hold to

Integrity

We are open about our university partnerships and always recommend what serves the student best.

Rigour

Documentation, deadlines and compliance are handled to the standard an admissions officer expects.

Candour

We give honest feedback early, so students have time to strengthen their applications.

Continuity

The counsellor who assesses your profile is the one who sees your application through.

How we got here

2010

SA Global Education founded, working with students in Karachi applying to UK institutions.

2012

Operations extended across Pakistan, with counselling delivered in Lahore and Islamabad.

2015

Formal university partnership programme established with UK institutions.

2018

Pathway provider relationships added, opening foundation and pre-master's routes.

2020

Counselling moved online during the pandemic, and remote sessions remain available today.

2024

Birmingham office opened, bringing UK-side support in house for arriving students.

2026

Expanding across Pakistan

Lahore and Islamabad offices open soon, with more branches to follow.
