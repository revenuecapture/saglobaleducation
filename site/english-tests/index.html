English language tests

Most universities abroad ask for proof of English. Here is how the main tests compare, which ones work for a UK Student visa, and how we help you pick the right one for your course.

The main tests

IELTS Academic

Scored 0 to 9 in half bands

The most widely accepted test for universities in the UK, Australia, Canada and New Zealand. Four skills: listening, reading, writing and speaking. IELTS for UKVI is the version approved for UK visas.

PTE Academic

Scored 10 to 90

A computer-based test of all four skills, accepted by most UK and Australian universities and many in Canada, New Zealand and the US. PTE Academic UKVI is the version approved for UK visas.

TOEFL iBT

Check which scale your university quotes

Widely used for US universities and accepted by many others. From January 2026 TOEFL iBT also reports scores on a new 1 to 6 scale, so compare your result with the scale your course uses.

Duolingo English Test

Taken online at home

Accepted by many universities in the US and Canada and by some in the UK. It is not on the Home Office list of approved tests, so it cannot be used where a UK visa needs a SELT.

LanguageCert Academic

Approved for UK visas

LanguageCert Academic SELT is on the Home Office list and is accepted by a growing number of universities. Check that your chosen course lists it before you book.

Skills for English UKVI

Approved outside the UK

A Home Office approved test delivered by PSI at test centres outside the UK, for applications that need a SELT.

English for a UK Student visa

Degree level or above: you need English at CEFR level B2. Your university can assess your English itself, so it may accept a test or qualification that is not on the Home Office list.

Below degree level, such as foundation and pathway courses: you need CEFR level B1, usually shown with a Secure English Language Test (SELT) from an approved provider.

Approved SELTs on the Home Office list include IELTS for UKVI and IELTS Life Skills, PTE Academic UKVI and PTE Home, LanguageCert SELTs, Trinity College London SELTs (taken in the UK) and Skills for English UKVI (taken outside the UK).

Exemptions: you may not need to prove your English if you are a national of a listed majority English-speaking country, or if you hold a degree taught in one of those countries.

Source: GOV.UK, Student visa: knowledge of English and approved Secure English Language Tests (list updated 5 October 2026). Rules change, so we check them again for every application.

What universities usually ask for

Requirements are set course by course. Many UK master's courses ask for IELTS 6.5 overall with no band below 6.0, or the equivalent in PTE. Some ask for more, especially in law, medicine and teaching.

Some universities accept other evidence in place of a test, such as English results in Intermediate, O levels or A levels, or a previous degree taught in English. Every course in our course finder shows its published English requirement.

Canada: institutions accepting an MOI letter

A Medium of Instruction (MOI) letter confirms your previous studies were taught in English. These Canadian institutions currently accept one in place of an English test for admission.

  • Dalhousie UniversityAll levels, conditions apply
  • University of VictoriaAll levels
  • Northeastern University (Toronto and Vancouver)Postgraduate only
  • Cape Breton UniversityAll levels
  • University of New BrunswickUndergraduate only
  • Lakehead UniversityAll levels
  • Ontario Tech UniversityAll levels
  • Trent UniversityAll levels
  • Huron UniversityUndergraduate only
  • The letter must be on your institution's official letterhead, signed and stamped.
  • It is only accepted if your studies were completed in English.
  • You may still need an English test for your study permit or for some programmes.

Updated October 2026 from the institutions' guidance for current intakes. Policies can change between intakes, so we confirm with the institution before you apply.
