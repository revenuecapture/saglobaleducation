An education consultancy built around every student's ambitions

SA Global Education is a UK-registered international education consultancy. We work with students in Pakistan and across the UK who want expert guidance at every stage of studying abroad.

Our counsellors give clear advice on the universities you can realistically join and the full cost of studying abroad, and help you prepare an application that meets the expectations of both the university and the visa officer.

Why students choose SA Global

Our advice is shaped by your profile and your goals. If a course is unlikely to serve you well, we will tell you and help you find a better fit.

Experienced counsellors

Your application is managed by a counsellor who has placed students at the institution you are applying to.

Clear, reasoned shortlists

Every recommendation includes the entry requirements, tuition fee, local cost of living and how the course fits your profile.

Thorough visa preparation

Financial evidence, credibility interview practice and CAS checks, prepared in line with current UKVI guidance.

Accommodation arranged before you travel

University halls or verified private housing, confirmed before your departure.

Practical pre-departure briefings

Banking, registration, GP sign-up, transport and settling in during your first weeks abroad.

Support throughout your studies

Help with course changes, work rights, visa extensions and the Graduate Route for as long as you are studying.
