Living costs and bank statements

Studying in London

£1,529 a month

£13,761 for 9 months

Studying outside London

£1,171 a month

£10,539 for 9 months

These are the living costs UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) expects you to show for a Student visa, for up to 9 months, on top of any tuition still to pay.

  • 28-day rule: the money must be in the account for at least 28 days in a row.
  • Never below the amount: the balance must not drop below the required total at any point in those 28 days.
  • 31-day rule: the 28-day period must end no more than 31 days before you apply for your visa.
  • Accepted evidence: bank statements, a bank letter, eligible savings accounts or other evidence UKVI allows.
  • Whose account: your own, or a parent's, legal guardian's or partner's account where UKVI's conditions are met, such as a signed letter of consent.
  • What it covers: any tuition still owed for your first year, plus the living costs above.

Source: GOV.UK, Student visa: money you need, checked October 2026. Rules change, so we check them again for every application.
